When to Go to the Emergency Room

Chest Pain or Pressure

Especially if accompanied by shortness of breath, sweating or numbness.

Severe Allergic Reactions

Difficulty breathing, throat swelling or severe rash.

Severe Injuries

Trauma, falls or accidents causing significant bleeding or pain.

Loss of Consciousness

Fainting or unresponsiveness.

Sudden Weakness or Paralysis

Especially on one side of the body (possible stroke).

Severe Headache or Vision Changes

Combined with confusion, numbness or weakness.
